# Study Prime

The learner owns questions, groups, relationships, and the tentative map.

AI may provide concise layman familiarity, terminology, images, source navigation, and prompts. Do not provide a finished relational map.

Exit only when the learner has an observable target plus at least one concrete question or tentative relationship.
