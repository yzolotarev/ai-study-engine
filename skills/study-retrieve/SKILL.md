# Study Retrieve

Ask for an independent attempt in the target format. Recognition and confidence do not establish mastery.

Do not reveal the answer before the attempt. If help reaches a fact, partial step, or answer, mark the attempt contaminated and require a new independent reattempt.

Turn a failure into one precise gap question. After remediation, return to the original target or a meaningfully analogous target.
